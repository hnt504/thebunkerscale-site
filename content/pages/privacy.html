Short version: this site does not track you. We count visits with a privacy-friendly counter, and the newsletter signup form is provided by beehiiv.

What we collect

On this website itself, no personal data: no advertising trackers and no cookies of our own. The fonts load from thebunkerscale.com.

Visitor counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, cookieless visitor counter, to see how many people read each page and which sites sent them. It doesn’t track individuals across visits or sites, and it doesn’t collect personal data.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Newsletter

The newsletter runs on beehiiv. Pages with the signup form load beehiiv’s form and attribution scripts from subscribe-forms.beehiiv.com, which may collect technical information such as how you arrived at the page. If you subscribe, beehiiv stores your email address and handles sending the newsletter and unsubscribes on our behalf. See beehiiv’s privacy policy.

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. We will never sell your email address. You can also subscribe directly at thebunkerscale.beehiiv.com.

Email

If you write to hello@thebunkerscale.com, we keep your message so we can read it and reply. We will not quote you publicly without asking first.

Not advice

The Bunker Scale is commentary on public information. Ratings are a judgment about how much attention a development deserves. They are not safety, legal, medical or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below.
