So... How Worried Should We Actually Be About AI?

Welcome to The Bunker Scale: why a 0 to 10 rating for AI developments, what you will find here, and why the shovel can stay in the garage for now.

October 21, 2026 · Read the full post

From background noise to get in the bunker.

The Bunker Scale rates AI developments from 0-10 based on evidence, real-world impact, autonomy, access, reproducibility, and how much the development actually changes the risk picture. It is not a prediction. It is a current reading.

  1. 0-2Background NoiseInteresting, but no meaningful change in risk.
  2. 3-4Worth WatchingSomething real happened. Keep an eye on it.
  3. 5-6Eyebrow RaisedA meaningful development worth understanding and following.
  4. 7-8Pay AttentionCredible evidence of a significant change in capability, risk, policy, or real-world impact.
  5. 9Stock the ShelvesA major escalation with serious practical implications. This does not literally mean buying seventeen years’ worth of canned goods.
  6. 10Get in the BunkerImmediate or rapidly developing catastrophic consequences that existing safeguards are struggling to contain.

How the ratings work

Our method

Trace the claim

Follow the evidence back to the strongest available source, not just the headline.

Label uncertainty

Say what is known, what is disputed, and what remains unclear.

Correct in public

If the evidence changes, update the rating and explain why.

Keep the temperature honest

Serious risk deserves serious language. Speculation does not become evidence because it is frightening or viral.

“Understand the thing you’re afraid of. Then decide what it actually deserves from you.”

AI can help with the work. It does not get to grade its own homework.
