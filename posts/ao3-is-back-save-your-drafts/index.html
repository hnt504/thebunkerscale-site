AO3 Is Back. Save Your Drafts Before November 29.

By The Bunker Scale · November 24, 2026

As of November 24, 2026. Still unrated: nothing official ties the outage to AI. This follows my first AO3 post and the November 6 update.

AO3 is back, fanfic folks. It came online again on November 15 after about 17 days down, a little sooner than the two-to-four-week estimate. There's one date worth putting on your calendar, though, and it's this weekend.

What's back, and what isn't

The Organization for Transformative Works (OTW), which runs AO3, posted "AO3 is now up!" on its status page early on November 15 (08:00 UTC) [1]. A few things are still switched off:

  • Icons for users and collections are off for now [1].
  • Downloads only work in HTML. Other formats are still disabled [1].
  • Importing works from other sites is also off [1].

On November 17, OTW reopened the invitation queue, so new accounts can be made again with invites [1]. AO3 went down again on November 19 for about 12 hours of what OTW called "preventative maintenance" and came back that evening, with the same features still off [1][3]. OTW hasn't given a date for turning them back on.

The part to act on: drafts

While AO3 was down, OTW paused its usual cleanup of unposted drafts. That pause ends soon. In OTW's words: "Beginning November 29, draft deletion will return to normal and all drafts which are 30 days old will be deleted" [1][2].

Plenty of drafts will be past 30 days by then, since the site was offline for most of the last month. If you've got drafts sitting on AO3, post them or copy them somewhere safe by Saturday. OTW's times are in UTC, so in the US, November 29 starts on Saturday evening.

Passwords and your data

OTW still says no AO3 data was deleted. On November 15 it said it has "no reason to believe that the user database was compromised, though we cannot rule out that possibility" [1]. So the old advice still stands: if your AO3 password lives anywhere else, change it there.

What you can probably ignore

"AO3 is down again, it was hacked again." The November 19 outage was planned maintenance, and the site came back the same day [1][3].

"The missing icons mean your account was wiped." Icons are switched off for everyone while OTW finishes the restore [1].

Bunker status: the shovel stays in the garage. Your drafts, though, need a new home by Saturday.
