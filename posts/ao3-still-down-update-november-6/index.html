AO3 Update: Still Down, Still on Schedule, and Those Old Emails Are Real

By The Bunker Scale · November 6, 2026

As of November 6, 2026. Still unrated: there's still no confirmed AI connection. This is a short follow-up to my first AO3 post.

If a pile of old kudos notifications just landed in your inbox and your first thought was "is this a scam?", that's a very reasonable instinct this week. Good news on that one, and a couple of other things.

What's new

Early on November 6 (00:00 UTC), the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW), which runs AO3, posted a short update on its status page [1][2]. The main points:

  • AO3 is still down. OTW says work to restore it "and make changes to prevent future incidents is ongoing and proceeding well" [1].
  • The timeline hasn't changed. "We believe that our original return time estimate remains accurate" [1]. That original estimate, from October 30, was a minimum of two weeks and possibly up to four, with a chance the site comes back first in a "degraded state," meaning some features missing or read-only [1].
  • Those surprise emails are real. While restoring things, OTW released some notification emails (like subscription updates and kudos) that were queued before AO3 went down in October. OTW's words: "These emails are real, from us, and are not a reason for concern" [1].
  • Drafts. If you had a draft saved on AO3, OTW says it knows people are worried and is "actively working to ensure that drafts are not deleted during this period" [1].

What hasn't changed

  • OTW still says no AO3 data has been deleted [1].
  • It still doesn't believe the user database was stolen, but still can't rule it out [1]. Nothing in the new update changes that, and there's no forced password reset.
  • No cause or attacker has been named, and nothing from OTW mentions AI [1]. You may see a "1 to 3 weeks" timeline floating around in some coverage. I couldn't find that in anything OTW has posted, so I'd stick with OTW's own wording [1][3].

What to do now

  • If you reused your AO3 password anywhere else, change it there. That's still OTW's main advice, and it still applies [1].
  • Treat the old notification emails as real, but treat anything asking you to log in, "recover" your account, or pay for access as suspicious.
  • Skip the mirrors and apps. In an earlier safety post, OTW warned people not to trust sites or apps claiming to offer AO3 access during the outage, said donations should only go through otw.org/donate, and said it doesn't recruit testers publicly [4].
  • Get updates from the source: the OTW status page or the AO3 News Tumblr [1][2].

What you can probably ignore

"Those kudos emails mean AO3 was hacked again." OTW says they were real notifications stuck in the queue from before the outage, sent out as part of the restore [1].

"AO3 is back in a week, someone said so." OTW hasn't given a new date. It says the original two-to-four-week estimate still holds [1].

Bunker status: the shovel stays in the garage. The drafts, OTW says, are staying where you left them.
